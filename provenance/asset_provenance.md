# Upstream asset provenance

See `UPSTREAM_ASSETS.csv` for model identifiers and unresolved revisions/terms. No upstream images or pretrained weights are redistributed. Recovered local source hashes are in `SOURCE_FILE_MAP.csv`. Author-created code, derived numerical outputs, third-party pointers, excluded material, and review-required checkpoints have separate classifications. Unknown historical tags and model-card/license discrepancies are not resolved by making this repository public.
