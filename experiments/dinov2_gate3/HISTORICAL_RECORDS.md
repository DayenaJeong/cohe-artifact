# Historical record preservation

The original mixed-provenance Ordinary 10% materials are not deleted or rewritten. In particular, the earlier addendum's historical rounded seed 0–2 Random records remain available for audit history. They are not used by statistics/ordinary10_clean_5seed_seed_level.csv.

The clean analysis supersedes those records for the rebuttal five-seed aggregate. This is not a claim that the historical runs were byte-for-byte recovered; seeds 0–2 were newly executed under the recovered seed 3–4 protocol.
