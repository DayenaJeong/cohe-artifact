# Release scope

This separated addendum contains derived scalar Top-1 values, paired statistics, selector indices, and cryptographic hashes. It contains no third-party images, datasets, model weights, checkpoints, latents, features, or raw logs. Users must obtain upstream CIFAR-100 data and any required model or software dependencies independently and comply with their terms. The addendum does not make a broader legal or end-to-end reproducibility claim.
