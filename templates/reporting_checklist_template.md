# COHE Reporting Checklist

- [ ] Proxy definition, checkpoint/model family, preprocessing, score direction.
- [ ] Target definition, classifier or endpoint, score direction.
- [ ] Matched sample IDs across proxy and target.
- [ ] Dependence evidence: Pearson, Spearman, density/running median; nonlinear check when feasible.
- [ ] Held-out predictive-validity evidence with split IDs and R2.
- [ ] Optional operational-transfer evidence with Random baseline, paired comparison, budget sweep, and diagnostics.
- [ ] Claim tier and permitted wording.
- [ ] Explicit boundary on claims not licensed by the evidence.
- [ ] Asset provenance and redistribution statement.
